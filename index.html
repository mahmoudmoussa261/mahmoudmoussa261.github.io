Case study

Gifteila: a gifting brand built by one marketer and AI

Gifteila sells ceramic mugs, espresso cups, bottles and gift bundles across Egypt. Its tagline is "Made to gift, made to stay." I built and run every part of the marketing myself, from brand and store to content, retention and email delivery.

What I built

Six systems that run the brand day to day

01

Brand & store

I designed the identity (logo, sage and terracotta palette, warm tone of voice) and the Shopify store, then set clear rules that build trust: cash on delivery with inspection, free shipping over 800 EGP, and WhatsApp support.

Branding · Shopify
02

AI product photography

Product visuals are made with AI image tools (ChatGPT, Gemini) instead of photoshoots, so I can list a new product the same day it arrives, in a consistent styled look.

ChatGPT · Gemini
03

Automated content system

I built a generator that turns live product data into branded Instagram carousels. It produced 12 designs for a full monthly calendar, with 18 posts scheduled across Instagram and Facebook.

Python · Metricool
04

Abandoned-cart recovery

I set up two automated flows (abandoned cart and abandoned checkout) with six branded emails in Egyptian Arabic. Each flow ends with a one-time 10% code for buyers who still haven't ordered.

Shopify Flow · Email
05

Email deliverability

I found and fixed broken DNS records (DKIM, DMARC), so store emails now send from the Gifteila domain instead of a generic Shopify address. More of them land in the inbox.

DNS · DKIM · DMARC
06

Social proof

I collect real customer reviews (4.79★ from 57) and reuse them in content and ads. I made a firm rule to use only genuine reviews and never invented ones.

Judge.me · UGC
